Pictayo

A private photo map and timeline. Pictayo turns a folder of photos into two things you can actually browse: a day-by-day timeline of when they were taken, and an interactive map of where. It runs entirely in your browser — no account, no upload, no charge.

How it works

  1. Choose your photos. Pick files or a whole folder from your phone or computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. Pictayo reads what is already there. Capture dates build the timeline; GPS coordinates already stored in the files place them on the map and name nearby landmarks and places to eat.
  3. Explore and share. Browse day by day, revisit places on the map, and export a standalone website you can keep or host anywhere.

Why it is built this way

  • Your library never leaves the device. Photos and videos are decoded and stored in the browser. There is no media backend to upload them to.
  • Only coordinates are ever shared. When a file has GPS data, those numbers alone go to place-name and map services — never the picture.
  • Nothing to sign up for. No account, no email, no tracking of who you are.
  • Made for every screen. Phone, tablet, or desktop, including touch and pinch on the map and lightbox.

The name

Pictayo is pronounced pic-TAH-yo. It comes from the Filipino and Taglish phrase “Pic tayo,” meaning “Let's take a picture” — the familiar moment when family or friends gather together to capture a memory. Pictayo is made for everyone.

Questions people ask

How is Pictayo pronounced, and what does it mean?
Pic-TAH-yo. The name comes from the Filipino and Taglish phrase “Pic tayo,” meaning “Let's take a picture” — the moment when family or friends gather to capture a memory.
Is Pictayo free?
Yes. Pictayo is free to use in a modern web browser. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no usage charge.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Photos and videos are read and stored in your browser and never leave your device. Pictayo has no media backend. When a file contains GPS coordinates, only those coordinates are sent to place-name and map services so a location can be named.
What does Pictayo do with my photos?
It reads the capture date and any GPS coordinates already in each file, groups the photos into a day-by-day timeline, and plots the ones with coordinates on an interactive map, naming nearby landmarks and places to eat.
Do my photos need location data?
No. Photos without GPS coordinates still appear on the timeline by date; they simply are not placed on the map. Most phone cameras record coordinates when location is enabled.
Can I share the result?
Yes. Pictayo can export a standalone photo website — a folder of your own photos and pages that opens in any browser and can be hosted anywhere or kept offline.
