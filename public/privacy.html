Privacy

Your photos and videos stay in your browser. PicturePicture has no media backend and does not upload your library.

What stays on this device

Imported media, derived previews, filenames, captions, and capture timestamps are stored locally in your browser. Browser storage is not encrypted by PicturePicture, so protect your browser profile and clear imported items on a shared device.

Location services

When a file contains GPS coordinates, PicturePicture shares coordinates—but never the photo or video—with:

  • BigDataCloud for place names;
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass services for nearby landmarks and dining suggestions; and
  • Google Maps when a map is displayed or you open a map link.

These providers also receive normal connection information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

Public-beta feedback

Feedback sends the category, text you enter, current PicturePicture page, viewport size, app version, and browser information to a dedicated feedback service. It creates a public issue, so do not include personal information, private photo details, email addresses, passwords, or links. Photos are never attached.

Your controls

Removing one item or clearing PicturePicture deletes only the browser-stored copies. Your original files are never changed, moved, or deleted.
