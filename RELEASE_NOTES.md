# Introducing Pictayo

Pictayo is a private photo map and timeline that organizes your memories by
when and where they happened.

## PicturePicture is becoming Pictayo

Inspired by the Filipino phrase “Pic tayo”—“Let’s take a picture”—Pictayo turns
your personal photos into an interactive timeline and map organized by date and
location.

The new name, Maltipoo mascot, colors, and website better reflect what the app
is about: capturing moments and rediscovering the places connected to them.
Pictayo is made for everyone.
